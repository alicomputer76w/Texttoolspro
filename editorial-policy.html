Editorial Policy

Our standards for creating accurate, helpful, and trustworthy content

Our Mission

Text Tools Pro helps creators, students, and professionals work with text more efficiently. We prioritize helpful, original content, clear explanations, and practical workflows that improve productivity and quality.

Content Creation Standards

  • Original writing with practical examples and step‑by‑step guidance
  • Clear, student‑friendly explanations and consistent formatting
  • Accurate descriptions of tool capabilities, limitations, and best practices
  • Internal links to relevant tools and guides to enhance learning

Review, Updates, and Accuracy

  • Key pages are reviewed quarterly; tools updated as features change
  • Corrections are logged and updated promptly when issues are found
  • We cite reputable sources (e.g., MDN, W3C, Google documentation) when applicable
  • Each page includes a clear heading structure and accessible content

Transparency and Ethics

  • We disclose affiliate links and advertising placements where used
  • Ads never obstruct content or core functionality
  • We avoid misleading claims and prioritize user benefit over monetization
